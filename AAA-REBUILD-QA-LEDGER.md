## P0 investigation and fixes — 2026-08-29

### Confirmed root causes

1. Grounded actors received a small downward displacement on every fixed tick. On a flat collider this accumulated while `grounded=true` until the capsule penetrated the floor. A fixed-coordinate 180-tick probe reproduced material penetration. Ground support now relies on the controller's snap-to-ground and keeps vertical velocity at zero while supported.
2. Mantle and swim-assisted mantle used centre rays to choose a target and then teleported the actor through a straight interpolation. Real NEO CITY and OLD FRONT stair candidates placed the full capsule in static geometry. Mantle now preflights the complete capsule in two segments and advances with the KCC; a blocked path aborts without penetration, launch velocity, or jump-count reset.
3. The authored capsule was 3.14 m tall while the rendered character is approximately 1.86 m. The standing Rapier capsule is now 1.99 m tall (radius 0.42 m, half-height 0.575 m, body-centre offset 1.015 m), aligning physical scale with the visible rig and hit regions.
4. Navigation stored authored platform Y instead of measured physical support Y, and narrow stair treads received no samples at 3 m spacing. Navigation now measures support, checks the full capsule, centre-samples narrow surfaces, and creates stair landing anchors.
5. Stairs were built as stacked solid pyramids. Upper flights occupied lower-flight headroom. Stair colliders now use thin tread/riser bands and retain continuous walkable support.
6. Window sill geometry was added beneath every wall gap, including actual exterior doors. This created an invisible/opaque 1.1 m barrier across windows-enabled entrances. Door openings and sill-bearing window openings are now separate inputs.
7. Glide, kill-plane, QA teleport, stress placement, and practice spawning could choose a ray-hit point without proving full standing clearance or connected navigation. They now use same-floor clear placement; practice candidates require navigation edges.
8. Swimming could evaluate the shore exit with the pre-move foot height and briefly report `ground` while ungrounded, retaining upward swim velocity. Shore exit now uses the post-KCC position and requires support, grounded contact, and full capsule clearance before zeroing vertical velocity and entering `ground`.
9. Cylinder and sphere colliders were smaller than their rendered meshes. Static round colliders now use matching Rapier cylinder/ball radii.
10. AI navigation mixed body-centre Y with feet-space navigation Y. Actor path queries and waypoint vertical steering now use feet space.
11. Glide touchdown and QA/bounds standing teleports invalidated `CharBody` contact flags but could retain or assign `state='ground'` in the same frame while `body.grounded=false`. Forced placements now remain in `air` for that frame; the following normal KCC tick must establish support before the actor can report `ground`.
12. NEO CITY's station and EDEN FACILITY's pump room were authored below continuous terrain surfaces. Physics and rendering now share an exact rectangular terrain-cutout mesh, so opening boundaries no longer leave diagonal half-cell caps or disagree between the visible and collision surfaces.
13. The first swim-entry splash could publish the actor's initial `-Infinity` water height to spatial audio. Water-surface state is now assigned before the splash event, and the event is covered by a finite-value regression.
14. Interaction range and floor checks did not require scenery line of sight, so an opaque wall could expose and open a chest or floor item. HUD selection and actual interaction now share a world/prop LOS gate that terminates at the chest's near face, avoiding both wall-through access and false self-occlusion by the chest collider.
15. OLD FRONT's quarry floor and elite chest were authored under a continuous sloped heightfield. The chest could be physically supported yet visually buried and unreachable. The quarry now has exact render/physics cutouts, a retained floor, retaining walls, a protected stair corridor, and a bidirectional capsule-clear route.
16. The standing camera/aim origin was `2.05 m` above the soles, above both the `1.86 m` rendered crown and `1.99 m` capsule top. It is now `1.72 m`, preserving feet-space LOS while matching the authored eye line; unit and four-map FP/TPS checks cover the corrected scale.
17. The attribution harness sorted frame deltas before calculating its supposed contiguous best window. Frame timestamps now remain chronological for sliding-window analysis, while a separate sorted copy supplies p95, p99 and 1%-low metrics; four deterministic unit cases protect the calculation.
18. Wall-run steering measured the complete horizontal velocity after storing its into-wall stick force. A blocked normal component could therefore be rotated into reusable wall-tangent speed on later ticks, matching the reported wall-adjacent jump-spam acceleration path. Wall runs now retain only real tangent momentum and apply stick force solely to the KCC displacement; a seeded wall/jump regression forbids the conversion.
19. Normal KCC landing captured `wasGrounded` and then tested `!wasGrounded` only inside an already-grounded branch, so the landing-reset path was unreachable. Direct wall-run landing also skipped jump-count reset, and wall-run entry never cleared `wallrunLanded`. Landing resets now occur after movement on the actual false-to-true contact edge, with the direct wall-run path carrying the same jump/fall/chain invariants.
20. The rebuilt chest combined indexed Torus/Sphere geometry with a non-indexed RoundedBox and passed the resulting null merge into `InstancedMesh`. Three.js then failed its render traversal every frame. Chest-part merging now normalises mixed indexing and rejects a failed merge descriptively before any GPU object is created.
21. Imported rock meshes were normalised to a zero local floor during asset extraction, but the world renderer added a positive placement lift afterward. Large boulders therefore visibly floated even though their authored collider was grounded. Rock instances now use a small scale-relative burial offset, and companion stones/scree stay inside the same proven collision footprint.
22. Weapon pop-out simulation updated loot X/Y/Z, while the renderer refreshed only Y. Dropped guns could therefore remain visibly at the chest despite pickup/LOS state moving elsewhere. World-loot transforms now copy all three authoritative coordinates and use grounded, fixed-scale presentation instead of floating/spinning objects.
23. Navigation searched only the immediately adjacent 8 m grid cells even though valid walk/jump edges may span up to 13 m. Clear neighbours separated by two cells were silently disconnected. Candidate lookup now derives its cell radius from the maximum edge range, with a cross-cell path regression.
24. Slide entry set `state='slide'` but continued through the ordinary ground update, which overwrote the state during the same tick. A successful slide now owns that tick and is covered through the next update with a non-penetration assertion.
25. Water queries applied the surface ceiling but ignored the authored lower depth, so points below a finite water bed could remain in swim/combat-water state. All consumers now share a complete finite-volume predicate and a shallow-water boundary regression.
26. Vehicle render aliases, scale offsets and approximate physics boxes were derived independently, leaving visible bottoms and roofs outside the collider by up to several decimetres. Renderer, placement checks and Rapier boxes now share measured per-GLB bounds, rotated local centres and one support plane; thin roof intersections are rejected by regression.
27. OLD FRONT lumber and firewood were authored as upright cylinders with their long axis buried below terrain. They are now visibly horizontal, locally terrain-supported stacks with matching rotated hidden collision boxes.
28. ASHARA was a production map but had no menu hero and the asset audit did not require map-card images. A reviewed in-engine 1600x900 hero was captured in the QA-only presentation mode, the four heroes are now mandatory assets, and a production-map test prevents recurrence.
29. Several browser harnesses could finish after a non-live state, empty/invalid performance samples, console errors, unapplied movement or a fallback teleport. The main all-map, POI, hero and performance gates now require live/grounded state where applicable, finite minimum sample counts, real displacement, resolved-placement proximity, explicit swim state and zero page/runtime errors.
